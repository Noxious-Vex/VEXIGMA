Contributing to VEXIGMA

Thank you for your interest in contributing to VEXIGMA --- Advanced
Cipher Laboratory.

VEXIGMA is a source-available project, not an open-source project.
Contributions are welcome under the rules below, but the public
availability of the source code does not grant rights beyond those
provided by the VEXIGMA License v1.0 and the VEXIGMA Contributor
License Agreement (CLA).

Before submitting a contribution, please read both documents.

1. Contribution Workflow

The intended contribution process is:

Fork the official VEXIGMA repository on GitHub for
contribution-development purposes.

Create a branch for your proposed change.

Make and test your changes.

Clearly identify material modifications where reasonably practical.

Submit a pull request to the official VEXIGMA repository.

Include the CLA declaration shown below in the pull request.

Disclose applicable third-party material and material AI assistance.

Respond to review questions or requested changes if you choose to
continue with the contribution.

A public fork permitted for contribution development is not an
independently distributable VEXIGMA release. Forks and modified builds
remain subject to the VEXIGMA License v1.0.

2. Contributor License Agreement

A contribution cannot be accepted into official VEXIGMA unless the
contributor agrees to the current applicable VEXIGMA Contributor
License Agreement.

Please read VEXIGMA-CLA.md before submitting a pull request.

Your GitHub account and explicit pull-request declaration may serve as
the record of your CLA acceptance, as described by the CLA.

Required Pull-Request Declaration

Include the following declaration in your pull request:

I have read and agree to the VEXIGMA Contributor License Agreement
(CLA). I confirm that I have the rights necessary to submit this
Contribution and that I have disclosed any material third-party or
AI-generated/AI-assisted content as required by the CLA.

A pull request that does not contain the required declaration is not
eligible for acceptance until the declaration is provided.

3. Ownership Before and After Acceptance

Submitting a pull request does not immediately transfer ownership of
your contribution.

Until material is accepted into official VEXIGMA, ownership remains with
you or the applicable rights holder, and you may withdraw the
contribution in accordance with the CLA.

When material is accepted into official VEXIGMA, the copyright
assignment and other applicable rights described in the CLA take effect
for the accepted material.

Please read the CLA carefully before contributing.

4. No Guarantee of Review or Acceptance

Submission of a contribution does not guarantee review, discussion,
acceptance, merging, credit in a particular form, or incorporation into
VEXIGMA.

NoxiousVex may accept, reject, close, modify, defer, or decline to
review a contribution at their discretion.

Acceptance also does not guarantee that a contribution or feature will
remain in future VEXIGMA versions.

5. What to Include in a Pull Request

A useful pull request should explain:

what was changed;

why the change was made;

how the change was tested;

any known limitations or unresolved issues;

any material third-party content included; and

any material use of generative AI or similar AI systems.

Keep unrelated changes separate where reasonably practical. This makes
review and rights tracking easier.

6. AI-Assisted Contributions

AI-assisted and AI-generated contributions are permitted, subject to the
CLA.

You must disclose material use of generative AI or similar AI
systems in preparing the contribution.

Examples that generally should be disclosed include substantial code,
documentation, algorithms, designs, or other project material generated
or substantially rewritten by an AI system.

Trivial assistance such as ordinary autocomplete, basic syntax
completion, or similarly incidental assistance does not require special
disclosure.

A simple disclosure is sufficient, for example:

AI Disclosure: Portions of this contribution were created or
substantially assisted using an AI coding tool. I reviewed the
resulting material and am submitting it subject to the VEXIGMA CLA.

The contributor remains responsible for ensuring, to the best of their
knowledge, that they have sufficient rights to submit the contribution
and that it does not contain unlawfully copied or incompatibly licensed
material.

NoxiousVex may reject AI-assisted material where provenance,
originality, licensing, or other rights are uncertain.

7. Third-Party Material

Do not submit third-party code, artwork, documentation, or other
material unless you have sufficient rights to do so.

If a contribution contains third-party material, clearly disclose:

what the material is;

where it came from;

who owns or authored it, if known; and

the license or other terms that apply to it.

NoxiousVex may reject third-party material whose licensing, provenance,
or compatibility is unclear or unsuitable for VEXIGMA.

8. Employer, Client, School, or Organizational Rights

If your contribution was created as part of employment, contract work,
academic work, or another relationship where someone else may own or
control relevant intellectual-property rights, make sure you have
sufficient authorization before submitting it.

Do not submit material owned by an employer, client, institution, or
other rights holder unless you are authorized to contribute it under the
VEXIGMA CLA.

9. Testing

Please test your changes before submitting them.

Where applicable, verify that the affected VEXIGMA functionality still
works as intended and explain the testing performed in the pull request.

If you know that something is incomplete, experimental, or untested,
disclose that clearly rather than presenting it as fully verified.

10. Security Vulnerabilities

Do not publish an unpatched security vulnerability in a public issue
or pull request merely to report it.

Security vulnerabilities should be reported privately to:

NoxiousVex1993@gmail.com

VEXIGMA uses a coordinated disclosure approach of up to 90 days,
subject to the VEXIGMA License and applicable law.

Additional security-reporting procedures may be provided in
SECURITY.md.

11. Branding and Official Status

Contribution forks, development builds, and modified versions must not
be represented as Official Releases.

Do not use VEXIGMA branding in a way that suggests your fork, build,
project, or service is produced, endorsed, sponsored, or officially
distributed by NoxiousVex when it is not.

12. Contributor Credit

NoxiousVex will make reasonable efforts to preserve contributor
acknowledgment through mechanisms such as Git history, commit
attribution, release notes, or contributor listings.

No particular placement, prominence, or permanent form of credit is
guaranteed, as provided by the CLA.

13. Compensation

Contributions are voluntary and unpaid unless NoxiousVex separately
agrees otherwise in writing.

Submitting or accepting a contribution does not automatically create
employment, wages, royalties, revenue sharing, partnership, joint
ownership, or other compensation rights.
